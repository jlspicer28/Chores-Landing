Delete Your Chores Account

You can delete your Chores account and request removal of your associated data at any time. This page explains exactly how and what happens to your data.

Option 1: Delete in the App (Fastest)

  1. Open the Chores app on iOS or Android.
  2. Tap the Profile tab (bottom right).
  3. Scroll to the Account section.
  4. Tap Delete Account at the bottom.
  5. Type DELETE in the confirmation field and tap confirm.

Your account is deactivated immediately and cannot be restored. Permanent data deletion follows within 30 days per our retention policy below.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you can't access the app or prefer to request deletion in writing, email us:

Email support@choresnearme.com

Use the subject line "Delete My Account" and include the email address associated with your Chores account so we can verify ownership. We will confirm deletion within 7 business days.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed within 30 days:

  • Profile (name, email, phone, address, avatar, bio, skills)
  • Job listings you posted
  • Job applications you submitted
  • Messages sent through the platform
  • Reviews you wrote (anonymized — the rating remains for the recipient's overall score, but your name is removed)
  • Saved payment methods (Stripe customer record purged)
  • Push notification tokens
  • Device and session data

What May Be Retained

Some data is retained to comply with legal, financial, and tax obligations even after account deletion.

Specifically:

  • Transaction records. We retain payment, escrow, and payout records for up to 7 years to satisfy IRS, financial-services, and audit requirements. These records do not contain your active profile information after deletion.
  • Records related to disputes, fraud, abuse reports, or safety incidents may be retained as long as legally required to resolve the matter or to protect the platform and other users.
  • Anonymized aggregate analytics (e.g., "Oxford had X jobs posted in April 2026") may be retained indefinitely. These contain no personally identifiable information.
  • Tax forms (1099-Ks, etc.) are retained as required by federal and state law.

Data Deletion Without Account Deletion

You can also request deletion of specific data without closing your account. Email support@choresnearme.com with a description of what you want removed (for example: a specific message, a job listing, or your address). We will respond within 7 business days.

Questions

If you have questions about account deletion or your privacy rights, contact us at support@choresnearme.com.

You can also review our full Privacy Policy for more details on how we handle your data.
